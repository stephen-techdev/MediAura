Get in touch
Do I need an appointment?
You can book an appointment online, call our reception, or visit the hospital in person. Walk-ins are seen the same day, though booking ahead guarantees your preferred clinician and time slot.
How can I book an appointment?
Use the booking form on this page, call +1 (800) 555-0123, or open the MediAura patient app. You'll pick a department, choose a clinician and receive a confirmation with directions and prep instructions.
Do you accept insurance?
Yes — we work with all major national providers and handle the claim for you. Enter your policy in the booking form and we'll confirm coverage before your visit.
Do you provide emergency care?
Our emergency unit is staffed 24/7 with rapid-response ambulances covering the region. Average triage time is under five minutes, and on-site imaging means faster decisions.
Can I choose my doctor?
Absolutely. Every doctor has a public profile with specialisms, languages spoken and available slots — you can request them directly when booking, or ask for the next available specialist.
Do you offer health checkup packages?
Three packages cover routine screening through to full-body evaluation with two specialist consultations. All are fixed-price and include a written results review with your physician.
